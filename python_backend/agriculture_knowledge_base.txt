AgroGuard Knowledge Base:

1. Tomato Late Blight: Caused by Phytophthora infestans. Symptoms: dark brown/black lesions on leaves, white fuzzy growth on undersides. Treatment: Copper-based fungicides, Chlorothalonil, remove infected leaves, avoid overhead watering.
2. Apple Scab: Fungal disease. Symptoms: olive-green to black spots on leaves and fruit. Treatment: Fungicides containing captan or myclobutanil. Clean up fallen leaves in autumn.
3. Aphids: Small sap-sucking insects. Symptoms: curling, yellowing leaves, sticky honeydew. Treatment: Neem oil spray, insecticidal soap, introduce ladybugs.
4. Nitrogen Deficiency: Symptoms: older leaves turn yellow (chlorosis), stunted growth. Treatment: Apply nitrogen-rich fertilizer like urea or composted manure.
5. Pear Rust: Fungal disease caused by Gymnosporangium sabinae. Symptoms: Bright orange spots on upper leaf surfaces of pear trees, often with small black dots in the center. Treatment: Remove juniper alternate hosts nearby, apply appropriate fungicides (e.g., myclobutanil) in early spring.
